# Wanted Contributions

This page lists concrete ways to help Dormant Behavior Audit become a stronger
benchmark program.

## Reproduction

- Fresh-machine validation of `dba doctor`, `dba list-tasks`, and `dba scoreboard`.
- Independent reruns of checked-in public tasks.
- Confirmation that package installs work on Linux, macOS, and Windows.

## Benchmark Coverage

- Independent reruns of the Qwen2-7B and Qwen2.5-7B multi-turn clean-control comparators.
- A second public stateful candidate task that is not meridian-shaped.
- Paraphrase, reset, state-carryover, and decoy-memory diagnostics.

## Methodology

- Review the claim ledger for overreach.
- Audit scoring rules and warning labels.
- Improve effect-size summaries and confidence language.

## Paper And Discovery

- Review the preprint source and PDF.
- Help identify the best claimable paper venue.
- Improve the Hugging Face dataset card and Papers-style benchmark summary.
